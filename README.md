## 🧠 About Me

I'm a tech professional passionate about turning complex ideas into real digital solutions.  
With a background in **Computer Science** and hands-on work in **software architecture**, **backend development**, and **DevOps**, I’ve built systems from the ground up that integrate code, data, and infrastructure.

🔍 Highlights of my journey:
- Started in agriculture and evolved to tech via precision farming
- Experience with software + hardware labs: circuits, microcontrollers, embedded systems
- Strong foundation in software engineering, AI, distributed systems, and database architecture
- Passion for turning **knowledge into automation**, and theory into action

> "I believe in building things that matter. Fast, clean, and always improving."
